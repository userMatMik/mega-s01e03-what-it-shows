The problems

parser: dates read month first

The shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants all write the day first, so a booking for 7 April comes out as 4 July. It reaches every tenant, through the app, by email and through both booking forms. This is the bug #4471 was opened for, and the fix that is queued.

causes.ts files booking-v3 form reports as a separate cause, hint: the form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. That is not a second problem. People who type month first, as the hint says, are read month first by the parser, so the hint alone cannot flip a date. Those reports show the same flip as every other parser report, and the same fix covers them.

shift: the booking lands one day off

Observed. These reports are not a flip. The date moves by one day (“Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”), bookings in one month come out a day off, the same meeting falls on different days in Lisbon and Toronto, and an invite arrives before its booking. A day/month swap cannot do that: it keeps the date or moves it to another month. The desk had already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), but counted them under parser by tenant.

Not confirmed. A time-zone or daylight-saving conversion of the invite would fit: different days in two cities, and a shift that only shows in some months. Nobody has checked the code yet. Look at how invite dates are converted between the tenant’s and the recipient’s time zone before calling it that.

How to read a report

  • Place a report by its tenant’s locale and by what its body says, not by its subject. The same subjects come in for every problem above.
  • Do not use the intake tag. It follows the reporter’s browser, not the cause.
